## What counts as commercial use

Roughly, anything done for commercial advantage or monetary compensation, including:

- Using Serenity inside a company or for work that is not a noncommercial purpose.
- Bundling, embedding, or redistributing Serenity (or a derivative) in a product or
  service you sell or monetize.
- Offering Serenity, or a hosted version of it, to paying customers.

Personal use, study, research, hobby projects, education, and use by nonprofit or
government organizations stay free under the PolyForm Noncommercial License.
