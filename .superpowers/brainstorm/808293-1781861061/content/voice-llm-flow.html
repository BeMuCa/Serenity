SERENITY · CaptureRouter

Voice → Local LLM → Structured Capture

Push-to-talk audio is transcribed locally by Whisper, then a small local instruct model (Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct, Q4, ~1.9 GB) turns free speech into a schema-constrained JSON object and routes it to a todo, note, or person/KB entry. Nothing leaves the machine. Pick a phrase below.
